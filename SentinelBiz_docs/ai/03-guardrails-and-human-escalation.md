# AI Guardrails and Human Escalation

## Guardrails
- Authentication and authorization before AI access.
- Input validation.
- Prompt-injection defenses.
- Retrieval authorization.
- Output policy checks.
- Sensitive-topic detection.
- Tool/action allowlisting.
- Audit logging.
- Rate limiting and abuse controls.

## Human escalation
The AI may recommend or trigger escalation when confidence, sensitivity, policy, or user-support conditions require human intervention.

## Explainability
Where AI influences a decision, the system should distinguish facts, deterministic rules, predictions, retrieved evidence, and generated language.
