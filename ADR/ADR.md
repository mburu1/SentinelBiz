# Architecture Decision Records

## Purpose

Record significant architecture decisions, their context, and their consequences.

## Decision principles

- Record decisions when reversing them would be costly.
- Capture the reasoning, not just the outcome.
- Link decisions to requirements and constraints.
- Revisit decisions when assumptions change.
