# Problem

## Purpose

Describe the business problem that SentinelBiz is intended to address.

## Problem statement

SentinelBiz needs a clear, shared definition of the problem before requirements,
design, and implementation decisions are made.

## Stakeholders

- Business owners
- Operations users
- Administrators
- Support and compliance teams

## Current challenges

- The current workflow and its pain points have not yet been documented.
- Business outcomes and success measures need confirmation.
- Constraints, dependencies, and assumptions need to be made explicit.

## Desired outcome

Provide a reliable basis for prioritizing requirements and evaluating whether the
solution solves the intended business problem.

## Open questions

- Who are the primary users?
- What process is being improved?
- What measurable outcomes define success?
- Which constraints are fixed?
